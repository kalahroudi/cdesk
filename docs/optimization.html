Optimisation approach

CDesk applies the advice from “2 Years with Colima: the optimization guide I wish I had from day one” by Miftahul Huda. Thank you to the author. CDesk is not affiliated with the guide or with Colima.

The 13 points

PointWhyHow CDesk helps
1. vz instead of qemuApple Virtualization.framework starts faster, uses less energy, and unlocks virtiofs and Rosetta (Apple silicon).Recommends vz and marks it with a lock badge.
2. virtiofs mountsvirtiofs instead of sshfs or 9p is the biggest speedup for npm install and builds.Recommends virtiofs and warns about slow sshfs mounts.
3. Know what is fixedVM type, mount type, architecture and runtime are fixed at creation. Changing them means delete and recreate.Lock badges, and a Recreate VM side sheet that shows what is kept and deleted, offers to export volumes, saves colima.yaml and the image list to ~/Colima Backups/<profile>/, and requires typing the profile name.
4. CPU, memory, diskCPU and memory change with a restart. Disk can only grow.Tells you which kind of change you are making and offers Apply & Restart.
5. Right-size the VMAbout half your CPU cores and 50% of RAM. Colima’s own default is only 2 CPU and 2 GB.Detects your Mac, recommends a size and shows a bar of what the VM takes from the Mac.
6. Rosetta off by defaultOnly needed to run linux/amd64 images.Only suggests it when linux/amd64 images exist.
7. mountInotifyFile-change events let nodemon, Vite and webpack work without polling. Colima marks it experimental.Turns it on and explains it.
8. Fixed DNS resolvers1.1.1.1 and 8.8.8.8 avoid breakage on Wi-Fi switches, VPNs and split-DNS networks.Sets them, and offers Repair, then Use fixed DNS & restart.
9. Kubernetes offLeave it off unless you need it.Off by default.
10. Named profilesA lean default, a heavy one for big builds, a minimal one for quick tests.Manages profiles and switches the Docker context (colima or colima-<profile>) in one click.
11. Keep colima.yaml in dotfiles~/.colima/<profile>/colima.yaml is your reproducible setup.The YAML tab shows it, with copy, open and reveal.
12. Know the exact commandThe same setup, from the command line.The Equivalent command box shows the exact colima start ... flags for the form, copyable.
13. Plain-language adviceKnow what to change, and how disruptive it is.The Optimize panel gives advice marked good, suggestion or warning, and says whether a change needs a restart or a recreate.

Sizing rule

Give the VM about half your CPU cores and 50% of your RAM. These are rough starting points; CDesk detects your Mac and recommends a size.

Your MacCPUMemoryDisk
8 GB RAM2 to 44 GByour choice

For comparison, Colima’s own default is only 2 CPU and 2 GB.

What needs a restart, and what needs a recreate

ChangeWhat it takes
CPU countRestart
MemoryRestart
Disk size (grow only)Restart
Fixed DNS resolversRestart
VM type (vz or qemu)Recreate (locked)
Mount type (virtiofs, sshfs, 9p)Recreate (locked)
ArchitectureRecreate (locked)
RuntimeRecreate (locked)

Rosetta, mountInotify and Kubernetes are listed in the Optimize panel, which tells you for each change whether it needs a restart or a recreate. Disk can never shrink.
